Privacy Policy — Rune Survivors

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Rune Survivors ("the App") is developed by dugongcode ("we").

This policy explains what information is collected when you use the App.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your

name, email address or location. Your game progress (gold, upgrades, records,

settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may

collect and process:

  • device identifiers (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • app interactions with ads, and diagnostic information.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized

ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform). On iOS, personalized ads are

only shown if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You

can change these choices in your device settings at any time.

See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect

information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because we store nothing on our servers, uninstalling the App deletes all game

data. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.

수집하는 정보

개발자는 이름, 이메일, 위치 등 개인정보를 요청하거나 수집·저장하지 않습니다.

게임 진행 정보(골드, 강화, 기록, 설정)는 사용자 기기에만 저장됩니다.

광고

앱은 Google AdMob을 통해 광고를 표시합니다. 광고 제공과 성과 측정을 위해
